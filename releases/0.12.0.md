# Office 0.12.0

Automatic Windows updates prepare and verify the exact selected Office payload before Headquarters stops running jobs. The installed maintenance configurator then waits for confirmed teardown, preserves the Office identity during installation, and reports completion for Headquarters to verify.

- `Program.cs` routes the authorized automatic-update handoff to the installed `Start-CSweetOfficeAutomaticUpdate.ps1` helper.
- The helper uses `CSweet.OfficeRelease.ps1` to resolve an exact version and validate bounded HTTPS downloads, checksums, archive paths, and payload version. Host certification and development signing remain required for hosted development bundles.
- A one-use browser handoff is exchanged for a bounded machine-bound preparation receipt. Installation requires a fresh handoff after Headquarters confirms teardown.
- Work resumes through Headquarters after a fresh heartbeat verifies the selected version and healthy capacity; an Office paused before the update stays paused.
- Office.Contracts is pinned to 0.11.0. Earlier remote Offices need the current installer once to obtain this automatic-update helper. No installed service or payload is patched by this source change.
- The Office version and contracts-pin bumps invalidate the development guest build fingerprint. New release guests must be built/certified through the normal platform workflow.
