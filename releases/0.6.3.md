# Office 0.6.3

- Remove the automatic seven-day expiry from certification evidence generated on Windows and Linux hosts. Newly certified assisted installations keep working without weekly recertification.
- Preserve provider/image identity matching, evidence integrity, live readiness probes, and explicit expiry/revocation enforcement (SEC-INV-10).
- Existing installations need a freshly certified 0.6.3 payload installed through an identity-preserving upgrade. Drain the Office and wait for zero active assignments first; restarting or reinstalling a 0.6.2 bundle does not remove its seven-day expiry.

Office.Contracts remains pinned to 0.7.1. No Headquarters or contracts update is required. Hosted bundles still require target-host certification and local development signing before installation. Identity and image-signing certificate lifetimes are unchanged.
